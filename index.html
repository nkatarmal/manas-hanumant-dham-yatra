About the destination

Saturday is the key day

Manas Hanumant Dham at Nava Katariya is a major devotional destination in Kutch. The temple's public website highlights Saturday satsang, special aarti and large visitor numbers. This directory focuses specifically on the transport services people use to reach the Dham from cities across Gujarat.

FAQ

Manas Hanumant Dham Saturday Yatra

How do I find a bus to Manas Hanumant Dham?

Choose your source city from the directory and review the available Saturday transport listings. Contact the organiser directly to confirm the latest departure time, fare and availability.

Which Gujarat cities have Saturday yatra services?

The directory is being built city by city and currently includes listings from Ahmedabad, Jamnagar, Rajkot, Keshod and Surat. More Gujarat cities can be added as new operators are identified.

Are these operators connected to the temple?

No. This is an independent community directory. The listings are provided to help devotees find transport; please confirm all travel details directly with the organiser.
